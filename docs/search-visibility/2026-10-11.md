- /guides/ontario-application-document-tracker

Added an editable document tracker for Ontario college and university applicants. It separates requests from receipt and submission, records who is responsible for sending each item, and supports distinct program deadlines. Worksheet data stays in component memory and is neither uploaded nor persisted.

The guide distinguishes OUAC and Ontario Colleges workflows and tells readers to follow current program, portal and offer instructions. It does not submit, receive or verify application materials.
