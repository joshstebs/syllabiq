- /guides/student-workload-planner

The page provides an interactive, browser-only workload calculator with editable task estimates, weekly capacity and available study days. It reports total estimated time, daily average and the capacity gap. It does not predict grades, completion time or academic outcomes.

The supporting guide explains how to decompose assignments, account for recurring work, respond to an overloaded week and verify dates against current course sources. It links to the syllabus deadline checklist and student organization app comparison.

No external benchmark is used for recommended study hours. Students enter their own availability and estimates because course demands and personal circumstances vary.
