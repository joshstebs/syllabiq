The Ontario directory lists 23 publicly assisted universities and 24 colleges from Ontario government sources. It provides name and type filtering, official application links and explicit non-ranking disclosure. It does not reproduce program claims or recommend institutions.

The syllabus checklist is a printable source-audit workflow. It does not claim that SyllabiQ extraction or integrations are infallible, and it directs students back to current course sources and instructors.

Source notes:

- Ontario reports 23 publicly assisted universities and 24 colleges.
- OUAC processes undergraduate applications and universities make admission decisions.
- Ontario Colleges describes a maximum of five program choices and three at one college.
- Institution and program details can change, so the pages direct students to current official sources.
